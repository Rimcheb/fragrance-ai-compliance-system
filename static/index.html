Reading the limit column. A limit of 0% means the material is banned in Category 4. Anything above 0% is the maximum permitted concentration in the finished product. Materials marked unregulated carry no IFRA Category 4 limit; they appear here only because their structure resembles a restricted material, which is a reason to look, not a finding.

Odor network

Every ingredient placed in an inferred odor family. Edges connect members of the same family.

Open this tab to build the graph.

What the edges mean. Families are assigned by keyword match on the ingredient name, falling back to SMARTS substructure patterns — not by measured odor data. Edges are drawn between same-family members and capped at 12 per family for legibility, so the graph shows how the heuristic grouped things, not olfactory similarity.
Data: IFRA Standards, Category 4 (fine fragrance). Structures resolved to SMILES via CAS and name lookup. Descriptors and fingerprints computed with RDKit. Not regulatory advice — verify against the current IFRA Standard before formulating.
